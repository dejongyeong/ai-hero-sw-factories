# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary pins down the words used for courses, purchases, and getting money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for one of their Purchases, with their reason in their own words. A Purchase has at most one Open Refund Request at a time; a learner may ask again after a decline.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, refund (when the ask is meant)

**Open**:
A Refund Request that no one has yet approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested

**Approved**:
A Refund Request that has been accepted, which produces a Refund.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request that has been turned down; no money is returned.

**Refund**:
The full amount a learner paid for a Purchase, returned to them when their Refund Request is Approved — never part of it, and never the course's current price. A learner loses access to the course when they get a Refund. A Purchase has at most one Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which the learner may make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window
